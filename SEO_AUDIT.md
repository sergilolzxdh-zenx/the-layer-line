## Internal linking

- Each pillar page auto-lists its child articles (`PillarLayout`'s `pillar-children` section) — pulled live from the content collection, not hand-maintained.
- Each article has a curated `relatedSlugs` list (2–3 per article) driving the "Related" section — chosen editorially, not auto-generated by tag-matching, so the links are actually relevant.
- Cross-cluster links exist deliberately (e.g. `print-quality/stringing` → `materials/petg-guide`, `calibration/retraction-tuning` → `print-quality/stringing`) rather than confining links to their own category.
- Homepage links to all 3 pillars, 4 editorial picks, both tools, and every article via the "Browse everything" section — no orphan pages at launch (verify this stays true as content is added).
